← BACK TO ALL PROJECTS

Design Notes

ROLE: CO-FOUNDER + SOFTWARE LEAD · STACK: NEXT.JS, REACT NATIVE, SUPABASE, STRIPE

An AI-powered property-management platform for landlords who rent by the room. I co-founded it and lead a 3-person team, owning the build end to end across frontend, backend and infrastructure. Web and iOS apps are in production; it runs our own two shared houses (9 and 10 bedrooms) and we are now building it out with 2 design partners.

  1. Every room is its own record — listings, applications, leases, rent and move-in inspections are tracked per room, so a shared house is clean data instead of one spreadsheet.
  2. Money on one ledger — every Stripe payment writes through to the books, and the dashboard reads its numbers from them, never from hand entry.
  3. An agent that acts through tools — the AI assistant answers residents, prospects and vendors from real records and drafts leases and replies; every write is a proposal a person approves.
  4. Now building (Oct 2026) — a property-owner portal with statements and reports, vendor payouts and refunds, listing syndication through official partner feeds, and the iOS App Store release.
